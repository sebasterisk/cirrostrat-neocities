about the team

4 june 2026

7135D as it is now was revived in the Push Back season as part of a team consolidation effort across the 7135 organization. Our founding members consisted of long-standing upperclassmen previously from teams E, F, G, and Z, coming together to strengthen Loyola Robotics as a whole. With a reinvigoration in notebook, CAD skills, advanced building techniques, and autonomous programming efforts, 7135D is attempting to advance how Loyola Robotics will understand the VEX V5 Robotics Competition for years to come.

In the Push Back season, we competed in a high-level setting with 7135Z in our organization's first Signature Event as well as qualifying for the MD State Championship. We put a top 10 skills score and advanced to the quarterfinal: but we're still looking for more. We hope to take what we learned in Push Back in order to achieve greatness in Override. We hope to see you on the fields!
